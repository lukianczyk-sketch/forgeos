# ForgeOS — by RL Forge Works

Secure, fast, Windows-friendly Linux with a built-in private AI helper.

- **Base:** Bazzite (Universal Blue / Fedora Atomic, KDE desktop: taskbar + Start menu)
- **Games:** Steam + Proton built in; Heroic for Epic / GOG / Amazon
- **Windows programs:** Bottles (Wine) — one click per program
- **Browser:** Brave (ads and trackers blocked)
- **AI helper:** Alpaca (on-device, private, offline)
- **Security:** read-only core, signed updates, one-click rollback, sandboxed apps (Flatseal to manage), full-disk encryption at install
- **Clean Sweep button:** "I think I've been hacked" — resets the system to genuine ForgeOS, removes hidden auto-start programs and remote-access keys, resets app permissions, forces new passwords. Personal files are kept; anything removed goes to `/var/lib/forgeos/quarantine/`.
- **GhostFinder — see who's watching:** one button finds cameras on your network, spy-camera Wi-Fi and Bluetooth trackers (AirTag, SmartTag, Tile...) away from their owner; hot/cold hunt meter, trip watch, background watch, plain-English "what do I do now". Nothing leaves the PC.
- **Family Mode — one switch makes this PC easy and safe:** big text, bigger mouse pointer and taller taskbar; every safety guard on; "Call for help" lets a family member see your screen and help through RustDesk — only after you click Accept, and only their RustDesk ID can ask; it texts them the number through your phone. Turn it off and everything goes back exactly as it was.
- **Always-On Guard — checks everything by itself:** every download is virus-checked the moment it lands (bad ones locked away, fake "invoice.pdf.exe" tricks flagged); every new email in Thunderbird is checked for scams and virus attachments; known scam and virus websites are blocked for the whole PC from free daily lists. Plain-English pop-ups with "Show why", one window to see and undo everything. Nothing leaves the PC.
- **Forge Helper — one private AI helper everywhere:** ask anything in plain English from the menu or by pressing Meta+H; it answers from 67 built-in help pages, runs the right ForgeOS tool for you (only after you say Yes), and quietly warns you about a full drive, a failed update or an old backup. It never shares anything — the AI runs on your own PC.
- **Drivers & BIOS — you never update a driver by hand again:** drivers update by themselves with ForgeOS and it tells you in plain words what changed; after every restart it checks your graphics, Wi-Fi, sound and Bluetooth still work and offers the way back if not; firmware for mice, docks and drives installs by itself, and a new BIOS is one tap (it finishes at your next restart, never forced). It also finds parts with no driver and picks the right ForgeOS for your graphics card.
- **Windows & ForgeOS on one PC — one click to switch:** install ForgeOS from a USB stick onto its own drive next to Windows 11; then "Restart into Windows" in ForgeOS and "Restart into ForgeOS" on the Windows desktop take you straight to the other one, no boot menu needed. Next power-on goes back to ForgeOS.
- **Moving Day — bring your things over from Windows:** your Documents, Pictures, Music, Videos, Desktop and Downloads, your bookmarks (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) and saved passwords come over into ForgeOS and Brave, plus a list of the programs you used with the free ForgeOS way for each. Reads the Windows drive read-only (BitLocker with your recovery key), or a USB stick packed on your old PC with "Pack my old PC". Never overwrites anything; the password file is erased after.
- **Drive Health — a warning before a drive dies:** every day ForgeOS reads each drive's own health report (NVMe, SSD, hard drive and your USB backup drive) and says Good, Watch or Danger in plain English, with how worn out each drive is. If a drive may be failing, a big warning asks you to back up today and comes back every day until you do; "Back up now" opens Pika Backup and walks you through your first backup. It only reads the drives.
- **Ransomware Tripwire — stops a program that scrambles your files:** hidden safety files in your folders and a watch on how programs change your files. If a program starts scrambling (encrypting) your files, ForgeOS freezes it within seconds, cuts the internet and shows a big warning: "Put my files back" brings them back from the Undo Button's hourly copies, "Remove the program" ends it and locks it away. Never pay a ransom. "Test the tripwire" lets you watch it catch a harmless pretend attacker.
- **Fake Tech Support Guard — stops the "your PC has a virus, call Microsoft" scam:** the moment a remote-control app (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, UltraViewer and 20+ more) starts, ForgeOS pauses it and shows a big red warning. While anyone can see your screen, bank, payment, gift-card and crypto websites are locked and your passwords are locked away. One press of Ctrl+Alt+K (or the "Kick them out" button) throws the stranger out, cuts the internet, closes the fake virus page and shows a checklist of anything they left behind. "Test the guard" lets you watch it catch a harmless pretend remote app.
- **Digital Will — if something happens to you, your family can get in:** answer a few questions (your trusted people, your accounts and what should happen to each, your password safe, folders to keep, a letter to each person), then ForgeOS makes one locked Emergency Envelope on a USB stick and in Documents, plus a printed "Letter to my family" that says how to open it. The opening code is printed on a code card — with the two-key lock it takes two people together. "PC unlock for family" adds a code they type when the PC starts, and a checklist sets up Google, Apple and Facebook's own "after I'm gone" settings. Nothing goes to the cloud. It is not a legal will.
- **Find My PC — private, no company account:** if your PC is lost or stolen, open the Find My PC page on your phone and tap Mark as lost. The PC shows where it is (from the Wi-Fi around it), you can ring it, put a "please return" message with your phone number on the screen, lock it, or lock it and shut it down. Money websites and your password safe lock by themselves. Normal days it sends nothing, and everything between the PC and your phone is locked with a key only they have. No remote wipe, on purpose.
- **Why Is It Slow? — one button, a plain answer:** in about 15 seconds ForgeOS checks everything that can make a PC slow and tells you in plain English, worst problem first: a program hogging the power, memory nearly full, a full or failing drive, too hot, power saver on, a big background job, too many programs at start-up, an update waiting for a restart - or that it is the internet, not your PC. Every problem has a fix button that asks first, and it remembers what was slow in the last 7 days. No fake "RAM booster" tricks, and it never turns off security to "speed up".
- **PC Report Card — how healthy is my PC?** one page gives the whole PC a school-style grade, A to F: safety guards, updates, drive health, speed, free space, the Undo safety net, backups, privacy, hidden devices and being ready for trouble (plus the battery on laptops). Each grade has one plain sentence and a "Raise this grade" button that opens the ForgeOS tool that fixes it. Once a month it keeps a card on the PC and shows what changed, and Forge Helper tells you once if the grade went down. "Save a copy" makes a one-page PDF for a family helper. It only reads - nothing is changed and nothing leaves the PC.
- **Show Me How — the PC shows you where to click.** Ask "how do I print?" (menu, Meta+Shift+H or Forge Helper) and a glowing yellow ring and arrow appear on the screen over the thing to click, with one short sentence. You click it yourself; the arrow moves on to the next thing. 24 built-in guides (printing, Wi-Fi, sound, bigger text, video calls, email...); for anything else the private AI may only point at words that are really on your screen. It only points - it never clicks, types or changes anything, it stops if someone else can see your screen, and nothing leaves the PC.
- **Plain-English Errors — what went wrong, in plain words.** When something goes wrong, ForgeOS tells you what happened, how serious it is and what to do, with one Fix it button. Error pop-ups get a "What does this mean?" button, programs that close by themselves get a card, and Meta+Shift+E explains the error on your screen or one you copied. 141 errors are built in; a fake "virus - call this number" message is called a scam at once. Fix it only does things from a short checked list, the private AI may only explain, What Went Wrong keeps 30 days on this PC, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Which image?

- `forgeos` — PCs with AMD or Intel graphics.
- `forgeos-nvidia` — PCs with an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 16xx / RTX 20xx or newer card (including RTX 50 laptops). Uses NVIDIA's open driver.

Both images have exactly the same ForgeOS apps and tools (shared in `recipes/common-modules.yml`). With Secure Boot on, enroll the Universal Blue key once: `ujust enroll-secure-boot-key` (password `universalblue`).

## Install (switch an existing Fedora Atomic / Bazzite machine)

```
rpm-ostree rebase ostree-unverified-registry:ghcr.io/OWNER/forgeos:latest
systemctl reboot
rpm-ostree rebase ostree-image-signed:docker://ghcr.io/OWNER/forgeos:latest
systemctl reboot
```

Replace `OWNER` with the GitHub account that hosts this repo. NVIDIA PCs: use `forgeos-nvidia` instead of `forgeos`.

## Install from a USB stick (next to Windows 11)

Best setup: ForgeOS on its own drive, Windows 11 on the other. Nothing on the Windows drive is touched.

1. GitHub → Actions → **iso** → **Run workflow** (it also runs on every version tag). When it is done, download `forgeos` (AMD/Intel) or `forgeos-nvidia` (NVIDIA) from the run's **Artifacts** and unzip it.
2. Write the `.iso` to a USB stick (16 GB or more — the ISO is about 9 GB) with **Fedora Media Writer** (free).
3. In Windows on the target PC: turn **BitLocker off** (or save the recovery key) and turn **Fast Startup off** (Control Panel → Power Options → "Choose what the power buttons do").
4. Boot the PC from the USB stick (boot menu key at power-on, often F12, F8 or Esc). In the installer pick the **empty second drive** and turn on disk encryption.
5. NVIDIA PCs with Secure Boot: after the first start run `ujust enroll-secure-boot-key` (password `universalblue`).
6. At first login ForgeOS checks that Windows is found and offers **Set up the Windows button**, which puts "Restart into ForgeOS" on the Windows desktop.

If the ISO build ever runs out of space on GitHub's free runner, install the free Bazzite ISO instead and use the rebase commands above.

## Verify an image

```
cosign verify --key cosign.pub ghcr.io/OWNER/forgeos
```
