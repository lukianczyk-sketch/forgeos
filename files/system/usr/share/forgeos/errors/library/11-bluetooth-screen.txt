# Bluetooth, screens and the desktop

id: bt-no-adapter
match: no bluetooth adapters? (have been |were )?found
match: bluetooth (adapter )?(is )?(not available|unavailable)
level: yellow
title: The PC has no working Bluetooth
say: The PC can't find its Bluetooth part. It may be switched off, missing, or need a driver.
do: Check for a Bluetooth or airplane key on a laptop. Driver & BIOS Autopilot checks for a missing driver.
fix: open:autopilot
example: No Bluetooth adapters have been found.

id: bt-off
match: org\.bluez\.error\.notready
match: bluetooth (is )?(off|disabled|turned off|blocked)
level: green
title: Bluetooth is turned off
say: Bluetooth is turned off, so headphones, mice and phones can't connect.
do: Turn Bluetooth on in the Bluetooth settings (or the Bluetooth icon at the bottom right).
fix: settings:kcm_bluetooth
example: org.bluez.Error.NotReady: Resource Not Ready
example: Bluetooth is disabled

id: bt-auth
match: org\.bluez\.error\.authentication(failed|rejected|canceled|timeout)
match: (pairing|authentication) (failed|was rejected|timed out)
level: green
title: The Bluetooth device did not pair
say: The device and the PC did not finish pairing. The code may not have been confirmed in time.
do: Put the device in pairing mode again (see its manual) and pair it from the Bluetooth settings.
show: bluetooth
example: org.bluez.Error.AuthenticationFailed

id: bt-connect
match: org\.bluez\.error\.(failed|inprogress|connectionattemptfailed)
match: br-connection-[a-z-]+
match: le-connection-abort-by-local
match: (bluetooth )?(connection|connecting) (to .{1,40} )?failed
level: green
title: The Bluetooth device did not connect
say: The PC could not connect to the Bluetooth device. It may be off, out of battery, too far away, or connected to another device.
do: Turn the device off and on, keep it close, and make sure your phone is not holding on to it. Then connect again.
show: bluetooth
example: org.bluez.Error.Failed br-connection-profile-unavailable
example: Connection to Headphones failed

id: screen-not-responding
match: (application )?.{1,60} is not responding
match: (window|program|application) (is )?not responding
level: green
title: A program is stuck
say: The program stopped answering. It may be busy and come back, or it may be stuck.
do: Wait about 30 seconds. If it does not come back, close it (Terminate) and open it again; unsaved work in it may be lost.
example: Application "LibreOffice Writer" is not responding

id: screen-display
match: (could not|failed to) (apply|set) (the )?(display|screen|monitor) (configuration|settings|mode)
match: (mode|resolution|refresh rate) (is )?not supported
match: (input )?signal (out of range|not supported)
level: green
title: The screen setting did not work
say: The screen could not use the size or speed it was set to.
do: Wait 15 seconds: it goes back to the old setting by itself. Then pick a different size in the screen settings.
fix: settings:kcm_kscreen
example: Failed to apply display configuration
example: Input signal out of range

id: plasma-panel
match: plasma(shell)? (has )?(crashed|closed unexpectedly|stopped)
match: (taskbar|panel) (disappeared|is gone|crashed|missing)
level: green
title: The taskbar stopped
say: The taskbar and desktop part of ForgeOS stopped. Your open windows are fine.
do: The Fix it button starts the taskbar again.
fix: askpc:restart-taskbar
example: Plasma has crashed
example: The taskbar disappeared

id: wallet
match: kwallet.{0,40}(failed|could not|denied|wrong password)
match: (could not|unable to) (open|unlock) (the )?wallet
level: green
title: The saved-passwords wallet did not open
say: The KDE wallet that keeps some saved passwords (like Wi-Fi) could not be opened.
do: It usually opens with your login password. If you changed your password, ask your helper to update the wallet.
example: KWallet: Unable to open wallet kdewallet

id: kio-failed
match: (could not|cannot|unable to) (enter|read|open|connect to) (folder|the folder|smb|share|network folder)
match: (the|that) (folder|share) (is not available|could not be found|does not exist)
level: green
title: A folder could not be opened
say: The folder could not be opened. It may have been moved or deleted, or it is on another computer that is off.
do: Check the other computer or drive is on. If it was deleted, Undo Button may get it back.
fix: open:undo
example: Could not enter folder /home/robert/Taxes 2025.
example: The share could not be found
