# Office, email and video calls

id: mail-server
match: (could not|unable to|failed to) (connect to|reach) (the )?(mail|imap|smtp|pop3?) server
match: (imap|smtp|pop3?) (server )?(connection )?(failed|timed out|error)
level: green
title: Email could not reach the mail server
say: The email program could not reach your email company's computer. The internet may be down, or their server is busy.
do: Check the internet. If websites work, wait a few minutes and try again.
fix: askpc:restart-internet
example: Could not connect to the mail server imap.gmail.com

id: mail-login
match: (imap|smtp|mail|email).{0,30}(authentication|login) (failed|unsuccessful)
match: (application-specific|app) password required
level: green
title: The email password was not accepted
say: Your email company did not accept the password. Some (like Gmail) want a special app password or a sign-in through the browser.
do: Show Me How can walk you through email set-up again. Never send your password in an email.
show: email
example: Login to server imap.mail.yahoo.com failed. Authentication failed

id: mail-size
match: \b(message|attachment|email)\b.{0,15}\b(too (large|big)|exceeds .{0,20}(maximum|size limit))
match: 552 .{0,20}(size|exceed)
level: green
title: The email is too big to send
say: The email with its attachments is bigger than your email company allows (often 20-25 MB).
do: Send fewer or smaller pictures, or send them in two emails.
example: Sending failed: message size exceeds fixed maximum message size

id: mail-bounce
match: (undeliverable|delivery status notification \(failure\)|mail delivery (failed|subsystem)|address not found)
match: 550 .{0,30}(user unknown|no such user|does not exist|mailbox unavailable)
level: green
title: The email did not arrive
say: The email came back: the address may be typed wrong or no longer works.
do: Check the spelling of the address and send it again. Be careful: fake "undeliverable" emails with links are a common trick.
example: Delivery Status Notification (Failure) - Address not found

id: office-recovery
match: (document recovery|recover(ed)? (the )?documents?|autorecover)
match: (libreoffice|soffice).{0,30}(crash|closed unexpectedly)
level: green
title: The office program is getting your work back
say: The office program closed unexpectedly last time and saved a recovery copy of your work.
do: Click Start Recovery (or Recover) and then save the file again.
example: LibreOffice Document Recovery: Due to an error, LibreOffice crashed.

id: call-no-audio
match: (others|they|participants) can.?t hear you
match: (your )?(microphone|mic|audio) (is )?not working
level: green
title: Others can't hear you on the call
say: The call program is not getting sound from your microphone.
do: Check you are not muted in the call. Show Me How can show where to pick the right microphone.
show: microphone
example: Others can't hear you. Check your microphone.

id: call-connection
match: (poor|unstable|weak) (network |internet )?connection
match: (internet|network) connection is (unstable|poor|weak)
match: (reconnecting|connection (lost|interrupted))\W*\.{0,3}$
level: green
title: The call's internet connection is weak
say: The internet is too slow or unsteady for a smooth video call right now.
do: Move closer to the Wi-Fi, close other downloads, or turn your video off to keep the sound.
example: Your internet connection is unstable

id: print-pdf
match: (could not|unable to|failed to) (save|export|print) (to |as )?pdf
level: green
title: Saving as PDF did not work
say: The PDF could not be saved, often because the place you picked is full or read-only.
do: Save it to your Documents folder instead.
example: Failed to save as PDF
