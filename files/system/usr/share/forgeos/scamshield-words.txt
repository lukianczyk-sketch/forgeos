That is a classic fake tech support trick.
threat ~ 3 ~ \b(arrest(ed)?|warrants?|lawsuits?|legal action|police|deport(ed|ation)?|jail)\b|social security number ~ It threatens police, arrest or legal trouble, or asks for your Social Security number.
