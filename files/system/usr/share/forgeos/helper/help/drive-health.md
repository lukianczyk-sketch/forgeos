# Drive Health: a warning before a drive dies
words: drive hard disk ssd nvme failing dying health smart bad sectors worn clicking backup
Drive Health checks every drive 10 minutes after start-up and every day, and tells you BEFORE a drive dies.
- Good: no warning signs. WATCH: early signs (a few bad spots, nearly worn out, too hot) - make sure your backup is fresh.
- DANGER: the drive may be failing. A big warning asks you to back up today and comes back every day until you do.
- "Back up now" opens Pika Backup. No backup yet? It walks you through plugging in a USB drive and making the first one.
- Some drives die with no warning at all, so keep a fresh backup even when every drive says Good.
- Open "Drive Health" from the menu to see each drive, how worn out it is and its temperature.
