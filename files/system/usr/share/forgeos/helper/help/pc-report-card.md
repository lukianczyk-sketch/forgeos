# PC Report Card: how healthy is my PC?
words: report card grade grades health healthy checkup check-up score overall how is my pc doing
Open "PC Report Card" from the menu (or ask Forge Helper "how healthy is my PC?"). In a few seconds it grades the whole PC, A to F, with one plain sentence for each part.
- It grades safety guards, updates, drive health, speed, free space, the Undo safety net, backups, privacy, hidden devices and being ready for trouble (Digital Will, Find My PC). Laptops also get a battery grade.
- "Raise this grade" opens the ForgeOS tool that fixes that part. The report card itself never changes anything.
- "Not checked yet" costs no points - press "Check now".
- Safety and drive health count double; one F keeps the overall grade at C or lower.
- Once a month it keeps a card on this PC and shows what changed. "Save a copy" makes a one-page PDF in Documents for your family helper.
