# Moving Day: bring your things over from Windows
words: moving day move transfer old pc old computer windows files photos bookmarks passwords copy new pc
Moving Day copies your Documents, Pictures, Music, Videos, Desktop and Downloads, your bookmarks and your saved passwords from Windows.
- Open Moving Day from the menu. It finds the Windows drive on this PC or an old drive plugged in by USB.
- Old PC you can't open up? Make a Moving Box USB stick in Moving Day, then double-click "Pack my old PC" on the old PC.
- Nothing on Windows is changed, and nothing here is overwritten. Your passwords go into Brave and the password file is erased.
