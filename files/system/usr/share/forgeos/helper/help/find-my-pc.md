# Find My PC: find, ring and lock this PC from your phone if it is lost or stolen
words: find my pc laptop computer lost stolen missing track locate where is ring lock thief police
If this PC is lost or stolen, your phone can see where it is, make it ring, show a "please return" message and lock it.
- Set it up BEFORE it is lost: open Find My PC from the menu, click "Set up Find My PC" and scan the code with your phone. Tap "Add to Home Screen".
- If it is lost: open the Find My PC page on your phone and tap "Mark as lost". Every 10 minutes it shows where the PC is on a map.
- Ring it (loud for 2 minutes), show a message with your phone number, lock it, or lock it and shut it down.
- If it was stolen: tell the police and give them the serial number (the setup shows it - write it down).
- Nothing is sent on normal days. Only your phone can read where it is (locked with a key only your phone has). No company account.
- A PC that is turned off, or has no internet, cannot report. It reports as soon as it wakes up with internet.
