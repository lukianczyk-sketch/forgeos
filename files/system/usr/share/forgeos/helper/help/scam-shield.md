# Check a message for scams
words: scam shield fake email text phishing link suspicious real legit
Scam Shield checks a message, email or link for scam signs. It never opens the link.
- Copy the message (select it, then Ctrl+C), open Scam Shield from the menu and pick Check what I copied.
- Or ask Forge Helper: is this a scam? and paste the message.
- Red means a scam: do not click, call, reply or pay. Delete it.
- Real companies never ask for gift cards, passwords or codes by text or email.
