# Drivers & BIOS
words: driver drivers bios uefi firmware graphics nvidia device manager hardware autopilot
You never update drivers by hand on ForgeOS. Drivers come inside ForgeOS's own updates and install by themselves.
- After an update, Drivers & BIOS checks that graphics, Wi-Fi, sound and Bluetooth still work. If something stopped, it offers to go back to the version from before (your files are not touched).
- BIOS updates: when your PC maker sends one, you get a note. Press "Get it ready" and it installs the next time you restart. Keep the charger in and don't turn the PC off while it runs.
- Firmware for mice, keyboards, docks and drives installs by itself and tells you.
- Some PC makers don't send BIOS updates to ForgeOS. Drivers & BIOS then shows your BIOS date and how to update it from the maker's website.
- Device Check (in Drivers & BIOS) shows any part that has no driver, like the yellow "!" in Windows.
- Open "Drivers & BIOS" from the menu to see everything.
