# Digital Will: lets your family get into your PC and accounts if something happens to you
words: digital will if i die pass away passwords family emergency envelope legacy contact estate
If something happens to you, the people you picked can get into this PC, your accounts and your photos.
- Open Digital Will from the menu. Step 1: pick your trusted people, your accounts, your password safe, folders to keep and letters.
- Step 2: "Make my Emergency Envelope" puts one locked file on a USB stick and in Documents, and prints a "Letter to my family" that says how to open it.
- The opening code is printed on a code card. With the two-key lock it is split in two, so it takes two people together.
- "PC unlock for family" adds a code your family types when the PC starts. Without it a locked drive can never be opened.
- Step 3: the company checklist sets up Google, Apple and Facebook's own "after I'm gone" settings.
- Nothing is sent anywhere. ForgeOS reminds you every 6 months to make a new envelope. It is not a legal will.
