# Forge Helper
words: assistant ai helper ask question meta h shortcut
Forge Helper is the one helper for the whole PC. Press Meta+H (the Windows key and H) or pick Forge Helper in the menu.
- Type a question in your own words. It picks the right ForgeOS tool and answers in plain words.
- It never changes anything until you click Yes.
- Thumbs up or down teaches it what helped. Everything stays on this PC.
- You can also press the Meta key and type: help and your question.
