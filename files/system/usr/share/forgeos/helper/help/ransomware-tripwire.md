# Ransomware Tripwire: stops a program that scrambles your files
words: ransomware ransom encrypted scrambled locked files decrypt tripwire bitcoin pay safe
Ransomware Tripwire watches your Documents, Pictures, Desktop, Downloads, Music and Videos all the time.
- If a program starts scrambling (encrypting) your files, ForgeOS freezes it within seconds and cuts the internet.
- A big window then offers: "Put my files back" (from the Undo Button's hourly copies), "Remove the program", or "It's mine, let it go".
- The internet stays off until you press "Turn the internet back on".
- Never pay a ransom: press "Put my files back" first, or call your family helper.
- "Test the tripwire" in its window runs a harmless pretend attacker so you can watch it get caught.
- Honest limit: work done since the last hourly copy may not all come back. Files on USB drives can't be put back.
