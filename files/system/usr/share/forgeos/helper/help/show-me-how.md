# Show Me How: the PC shows you where to click
words: show me how guide walk me through where do i click arrow point teach me step by step tutorial
Open "Show Me How" from the menu, press Meta+Shift+H, or ask Forge Helper "show me how to print". Pick what you want to do.
- A yellow arrow and ring point at the thing to click, with one short sentence. You click it yourself; the arrow moves on.
- It only points. It never clicks, types or changes anything for you, and it never reads passwords.
- The small Show Me How box has Next, Back and Stop. Esc in that box stops it too.
- If it can't see the thing, it says so and shows the steps in writing.
- 24 built-in guides: printing, Wi-Fi, sound, bigger text, video calls, email and more. For anything else the private AI helps, but it may only point at words that are really on your screen.
- It stops by itself if someone else can see or control your screen.
