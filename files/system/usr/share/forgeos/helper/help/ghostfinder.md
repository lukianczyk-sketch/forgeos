# GhostFinder
words: ghostfinder hidden camera tracker airtag spy device bugged followed
GhostFinder looks for hidden cameras, trackers like AirTags, and strange devices on your network.
- Open GhostFinder from the menu and press the sweep button (about 30 seconds).
- Red items need a look. Hunt it shows hot or cold as you walk around to find a tracker.
- Bluetooth must be on to find trackers.
